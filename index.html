Pastor and Prophet B. Thomas Fallon

Founding Executive Pastor
Headmaster, OMEGA Leadership Academy

Pastor and Prophet B. Thomas Fallon is the founding executive pastor of The OMEGA Church and headmaster of OMEGA Leadership Academy. An ordained pastor and a retired teacher, he has given his life to two callings that meet in one place: preaching the Word of God and forming the next generation of leaders.

His teaching equips believers to walk in freedom, discernment, and the power of Holy Spirit, while helping to build people up in their most holy faith. "We must live and love in the truth of Scripture." Pastor Thomas has an extraordinary testimony, and God continues to work with him to help others honor God's design and order of creation, being reborn, renewed, and transformed by the Spirit of God in Christ Jesus.

Contact: ProphetThomasFallon@gmail.com

Ambassador Gloria A. Fallon

Founding Senior Pastor

Ambassador Gloria A. Fallon is the founding senior pastor of The OMEGA Church, known as The Gathering. For twenty-five years she has served in bi-vocational ministry.

Pastor Gloria credits the fear of the Lord as the beginning of all wisdom, knowledge, and understanding, and an unrestricted relationship with Holy Spirit for the approved study and guidance into all truth and the education she obtains. She claims no elaborate titles from mainstream education, only gratitude for access to historical documentation and for all those who have gone before us, making straight the path of the Lord. She has sat under the teaching of the late Dr. Myles Munroe and the late Apostle Jerome Bouverette. "I am an eternal student of Holy Spirit, and may the Lord bless whomever He teaches us through."

She is the author of many books, articles, and publications, including Encounters and Deliver Us! She speaks at conferences, retreats, and keynote events with a message of seeking first the Kingdom of God and His Righteousness, bringing teaching on:

  • The Solemn Divide: The Church vs. God's Kingdom
  • Powerwalking: Living with Holy Spirit
  • The Necessity of Deliverance Ministry: And the Ploy of hell to Discredit It
  • The Edification of the Body of Christ
  • The Necessity of Communication, Part 1: The Deaf Church
  • The Necessity of Communication, Part 2: The Powerless Church
  • The Kingdom Christian
  • Women AND Ministry

What we, The OMEGA Church, believe

The OMEGA Church is assembled in the honor of the risen Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, who was, and is, and is to come. Jesus is the Head of the Church, and it is to Him that the Church belongs. We are established by His will and Word, the leadership of His Spirit, and His continued command.

We believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, His only begotten Son, begotten, not made, our Lord, who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the then virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into heaven and is seated at the right hand of God the Father Almighty; He will come again to judge the living and the dead.

We believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy catholic (worldwide) Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and the life everlasting.

We believe Jesus has always been present, and Holy Spirit has always been present, with the Father, and that the Name of Jesus (Yeshua) has been revealed to us as the Messiah, our Savior.

We believe in the fivefold ministry gifts that Holy Spirit distributes. We believe in the leadership and the indwelling of Holy Spirit. We believe in the gift of tongues. We believe in prophecy, the prophetic, the apostolic, the work of evangelism, missions, and preaching and teaching for the sake of salvation and the increase of the Word and will of God in the earth. We believe in the power of God within our lives, even to produce miracles, signs, and wonders, and we are witnesses of the glory of God. We believe God is the same now and forever, and even if the cultures of the world change, He does not. We believe God is the Father, God is the Son, and God is the Holy Spirit: three distinct persons of God, yet He is one, and He is God. We believe in the Kingdom of God, His power reigning in our lives here on earth as it is in heaven. We believe in the love of God.

We believe in the use of historical documents, historical records, and verifiable accounts, and that the Holy Bible contains the infallible words of our God.

We have witnessed the existence of the kingdom of darkness, fallen angels, demonic beings, and satan. We believe that Jesus has imputed righteousness and authority to those who receive His free gift of salvation and the baptism of His Holy Spirit. This power, authority, and right standing in and with God restores the rightful order and puts satan and the kingdom of darkness under our feet. We believe in the power and sovereignty of the One True God, above every other god, lord, king, and government, and we are a sovereign nation because of the sovereignty of our God. Hallelujah!

We believe Jesus is the way, the truth, and the life, and that no man comes to the Father except through Him.

We believe there is no salvation except through Jesus Christ.

We believe in praying to God the Father, and that God the Son, Jesus the Christ, and Holy Spirit have made and continue to make intercession for us. We believe that prayer is communication with God, and that God also communicates with us. The Church is built upon the restored communication of God, revealing Himself and speaking to His people, and upon us, the people of God, seeking Him and hearing from Him. We must be, and we are, a house of prayer, set apart for the sacred worship of our God.

Non-discrimination policy

The OMEGA Church is committed to providing a safe, inclusive, and equitable educational environment that fosters respect for all members of our community. In accordance with federal, state, and local laws, The OMEGA Church does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, ancestry, national origin, nationality, immigration status, ethnicity, ethnic group identification, age, religion, marital or parental status, pregnancy, physical or mental disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender, gender identity, gender expression, or any other characteristic protected by law. Wherever state or federal law is in conflict with God and His written Word, please be advised: we are the Church, God's Church. Jesus is God. God is Sovereign. We always side with God.
